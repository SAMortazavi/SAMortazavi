<h2 align="center">01 / Academic research</h2>
<p align="center"><sub>NEURAL SIGNALS · COMPUTATIONAL NEUROSCIENCE · MACHINE LEARNING</sub></p>

### M.Sc. · Sharif University of Technology

**Electrical Engineering (Bioelectric)** · 2023–2025 · **Rank #1 / 17** · **GPA 19.02 / 20** · **Thesis 20 / 20**

> **Eye Tracking Using EEG Signals During Simultaneous Functional Magnetic Resonance Imaging**
>
> Investigated gaze decoding from EEG with simultaneous EEG–fMRI preprocessing, artifact handling, temporal/spectral features, and participant-specific ML models. The study included out-of-scanner EEG from **20 participants**, alongside preliminary in-scanner analysis.

**Selected participant-specific results:** **99%** passive viewing · **94%** three-class gaze · **~86%** five-class gaze.

<sub>These figures describe the strongest mean results among the evaluated models using within-participant held-out trials. They do **not** establish cross-participant or cross-session generalization.</sub>

### B.Sc. · Amirkabir University of Technology

**Biomedical Engineering (Bioelectric)** · 2019–2023 · **Rank #1 / 121** · **GPA 19.07 / 20** · **Thesis 20 / 20**

> **Age-related differences in Steady-State Visual Evoked Potentials (SSVEPs)**
>
> Studied visual-evoked EEG responses across stimulation frequencies using signal-processing methods, statistical tests, and regression-based analysis.

**Additional research:** Contributed to experimental design and human-memory/fMRI workflows at Ghazizadeh Lab, Sharif University of Technology.

**Speech recognition & data pipelines**  
Processed and curated large-scale speech and conversational datasets; approximately **50,000 hours of speech** were used for model training. Built and evaluated Persian and bilingual ASR pipelines, including noisy call-center conditions.

**Call-center quality intelligence**  
Independently designed and delivered a multimodal quality-control system processing **1,000+ calls/day**. It combines speech recognition, speech-emotion analysis, LLM-assisted transcript analytics, tagging, quality indicators, summaries, cross-talk/silence detection, and API integration.

**Multilingual document intelligence**  
Built Persian/Arabic/English OCR preprocessing and text-localization components, evaluation workflows, and FastAPI interfaces.

**Robust audio watermarking**  
Developed and tested a 16-bit synthesized-speech watermark against noise, cropping, resampling, compression, speed changes, and pitch shifts.
